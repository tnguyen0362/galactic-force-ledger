## V3-14 — DEFINITION OF HALF-ASSED, AND AUTOMATIC FAILURE CONDITIONS

The following are **categorical failures**, regardless of assertion pass count, project organization, or how many script lines exist:

- UI composed mainly of text labels/buttons with no cohesive diegetic environment, navigation, actual backend transactions or art direction.
- A single replayed mission renamed three times, or only one working mission where three are required.
- One functional enemy with differently colored skins passed off as five distinct tactical archetypes.
- Primitive Part-stack default Roblox characters/aliens, T-poses, broken rigging, visibly unpolished terrain or generic gray box station.
- Fake war-table statistics, dummy queue entries, hardcoded reward numbers shown as live data or dummy Priority Dispatch.
- An Armory with cards but no valid ownership/purchase/equip/use behavior; cosmetic or inactive buttons represented as functional.
- Static slides, plain text title cards or unvoiced slideshows represented as a cinematic 85–95-second authored introduction.
- No functioning tutorial, no extraction gunship or extraction reward logic, or no 1–4-player real cooperative operations.
- No controller-only or actual touch interface coverage; missing actions on one input family.
- Placeholder or missing on-screen interactions, no authentic combat FX/audio, gray terrain, clipping alien/weapon models, or incorrect camera placement.
- Using `369 automated assertions pass` or `server says true` as the *primary proof* that the game is built.
- Writing `feature complete`, `phase completed`, `MVP shipped` or `launch ready` while even one required launch item lacks demonstrated evidence.
- Leaving completed UI/visuals uninspected, or failing to name obvious defects in screenshot review.
- Summarizing the canonical spec into a few lines and implementing only the summary.
- Unilateral scope exclusions, self-written blockers without actual tool errors, unexplained missing assets, or owner acceptance forged by the harness.

A screenshot of a nice UI is not proof a button works; the human-played interaction needs a before/action/after capture. A slick opening video is not proof a functional campaign exists. A backend unit test is not proof a cinematic plays. All three dimensions—**functional, visual, and owner-approved**—are required.

# 1. DEFINITIVE PRODUCT IDENTITY

**Genre:** 1–4-player PvE military sci-fi third-person mission shooter with extraction, light scavenging, persistent progression and community Galactic War. It is NOT a PvP extraction shooter, tactical realism simulator, Minecraft survival game, MMORPG, or a fixed four-mission cave crawler.

**One-sentence store pitch:** “Deploy as an elite Galactic Force soldier, fight an intelligent alien army across ever-changing planets, recover enemy technology, escape with your squad's salvage, and help humanity reclaim the stars.”

**Three emotional promises:** (a) You are part of humanity's serious interstellar counteroffensive; (b) every deployment is familiar enough to learn but geographically and tactically different; (c) your personal equipment and the whole community's war effort advance through play.

**Core player verbs:** prepare, deploy, move quickly, aim, shoot, dodge, use two abilities, coordinate, complete one main objective, opportunistically scavenge, defeat rare elite/field-boss threats, call extraction, survive, upgrade, repeat.

**Aesthetic:** premium stylized low-poly with believable military proportions, large angular geological forms and smooth gameplay traversal, simplified foliage, coherent material palette, atmospheric distant mountains, cinematic skies/fog, original armored soldiers and aliens, readable silhouettes. Not Minecraft cubes, pixel art, or realistic high-poly meshes. No cartoon comedy. Serious, disciplined, inspiring, tense but not excessively bleak. No gratuitous gore; comply with Roblox content-maturity and platform rules.

**Duration:** normal operations approximately 10–25 minutes including extraction. Short ~10–12, standard ~15–18, extended ~20–25. Difficulty raises danger, not necessarily duration. Special boss missions separately tuned; 8-player community raid events are a FUTURE expansion, not part of initial core build.

**Spaces:** a shared, populated orbital social-station lobby is a functional hub, not an entire life-simulation feature. Players use armory, war table, Priority Dispatch, cosmetics and deployment bays. Missions happen in separate generated regions. No player homes, crafting minigames, economy speculation, survival hunger or thirst.

**Canonical names:** Galactic Force (human military); Ascendancy (launch enemy faction); Commander Elara Voss (female human Director of Field Operations); Galactic War (shared strategic layer); Priority Dispatch (manual emergency reinforcement board); Operations (missions); Credits (cash); Scrap, Energy Cells, Military Technology (salvage); Augments (equipment modifiers); Military Rank (account XP); Weapon Mastery (per weapon); Service Commendations (repeatable dispatch assistance reward). `Galactic Force: Counteroffensive` is the working game title; use consistently until changed by the owner.

# 2. CANONICAL LORE AND NARRATIVE DIRECTION

**Timeline:** The present setting is approximately four millennia after the beginning of humanity's spacefaring age, in an intentionally distant interstellar future. Twenty-seven years before the present campaign, the Ascendancy launched a surprise offensive through human core systems and invaded Earth. Earth was occupied for years, liberated by the coordinated human fleet six years before current gameplay, and has become the center of the rebuilt Galactic Force. Humanity now launches a sustained counteroffensive against Ascendancy-held colonies and frontier worlds. This makes the creator's “three-to-four thousand years” future feel coherent without implying an implausible uninterrupted four-thousand-year battle for one planet. Avoid showing an explicit calendar date on-screen; show “THE INVASION,” “THE RECLAMATION,” and “THE COUNTEROFFENSIVE.”

**The Ascendancy:** intelligent expansionist alien military civilization, not monsters and not generic robots; humanoid but unmistakably alien anatomy, large armored shoulder silhouettes, distinct elongated helmets and angular faceplate masks, layered ceramic-metal armor with purposeful luminous energy seams, orderly infantry and command organization, advanced energy weapons, fortified installations, and meaningful tactical coordination. Their motivations are imperial territorial supremacy and resource control rather than random destruction. Their soldiers believe human systems were strategically destined for subjugation. Present them as an organized threatening civilization, not comedy villains. Their unique motifs: obsidian slate armor, pale ivory rank plates, muted bronze industrial details, cold amber-violet light, triangular/offset-chevron military iconography and split-spire structures. Galactic Force visuals instead emphasize graphite navy, worn off-white, military steel, and restrained cyan-blue HUD/light accents. No copying Covenant, Automatons, Cabal, etc.

**Galactic Force soldiers:** trained planetary assault troops, capable but vulnerable, not disposable jokes; equipped with rechargeable combat shields, rifles, modular support equipment and atmospheric drop systems. A soldier can solo or join a 1–4 squad. Rank denotes operational clearance, not a power fantasy that invalidates beginners. Soldiers are backed by a coherent command network.

**Commander Elara Voss:** an actual human female operational commander communicating over the field network, supported by onboard targeting intelligence; do not call her an artificial intelligence. Voice: mature, firm, composed, precise, serious, humane, occasional understated praise; never sarcastic, overtalkative, goofy, or melodramatic. She is the consistent narrative voice during opening, tutorial, objectives, extraction, reinforcements and Galactic War updates. Build a subtitle-first event-driven dialogue system using original, rights-cleared recordings or locally produced licensed synthetic speech assets if accessible; text/subtitles and audio cues must function with voice assets missing. Never require a runtime external generative voice API.

**Story delivery:** no long linear campaign. Use initial cinematic, brief voice lines, mission briefings, strategic war-table news, changing planet status, named commanders and boss operations, and occasional major community events. All choices affect the shared territory model, while permanent unlocks remain safe across campaign setbacks.

# 3. OPENING CINEMATIC — SCRIPT AND SHOT REQUIREMENTS

Create a first-login cinematic approximately **85–95 seconds** (skippable; replayable via station Archive; per-account watched flag). It should be rendered using original Roblox scenes/camera tracks and optimized animated assets. Favor powerful composition, fog, lighting, scale, camera motion, sound editing and clean transitions over expensive mesh detail. Preserve readable subtitles, reduced-motion alternatives and a skip hold input on all platforms. Avoid copyright music. Final timing may shift by a few seconds for natural dialogue but narrative order and exact central beats are mandatory.

**0–10 s — ‘THE AGE OF EXPANSION’.** Start black with low-frequency starship ambience. Fade into Earth's blue curvature; a small formation of human exploration ships crosses frame; distant stations and colony imagery in rapid, elegant montage. Voss, low and controlled: “We crossed the stars believing distance would keep our home safe.” Camera slow, confident; music sparse strings/synth.

**10–25 s — ‘THE FIRST STRIKE’.** Cut instantly to dark silhouettes appearing outside Earth orbit, their alien hulls geometrically distinct. Signal interference, cascading emergency displays, distant flashes, orbital weapons striking military defenses, fragmented city skyline silhouettes (non-graphic), ships breaking formation. Voss: “Then the Ascendancy came. Without warning. Without surrender terms. Their fleets shattered our defenses and carried the war to Earth.” Use 3–5 short cinematic shots rather than heavy real-time simulation.

**25–40 s — ‘EARTH OCCUPIED’.** Smoke-shrouded coastline, human evacuation, alien patrol silhouette in ruined city, a Galactic Force banner lowered; distant surviving soldier holds a battered insignia. Voss: “Our worlds fell. Our fleets broke. Even Earth passed into enemy hands.” Keep violence stylized and primarily environmental.

**40–57 s — ‘WE HELD’.** Refit hangars, welders, armored troopers gathering, hangar doors opening, human ships leaving orbit, tactical display changing from red sectors to blue; first contact with resilient human units. Voss: “We did not disappear. We rebuilt our fleets. Reclaimed our strength. And fought for every kilometer.” Music begins accelerating.

**57–70 s — ‘EARTH RECLAIMED’.** Human soldiers breach a fortified Ascendancy station; massive orbital fleet emerges into sunrise above Earth; enemy command emblem goes dark on holo-map; Galactic Force flag rises over skyline. Voss: “Six years ago, Earth was ours again.” Hold a brief solemn beat; distant triumphant orchestral note.

**70–86 s — ‘THE COUNTEROFFENSIVE’.** Orbiting operations station, a large illuminated war table showing contested systems; close shots of a helmet clicking shut, assault rifle chambering, two energy support tools being armed, three soldiers boarding a drop craft. Rapid shots of orbital descent, alien valley, Ascendancy outpost. Voss: “But millions still live beneath Ascendancy occupation. Earth was not our final victory. It was the beginning of their defeat.”

**86–95 s — TITLE AND HANDOFF.** Wide fleet silhouette behind a planet. Display original title **GALACTIC FORCE: COUNTEROFFENSIVE**, clean institutional typography. Voss: “Galactic Force. The counteroffensive begins now. Report for deployment.” Transition seamlessly to tutorial drop sequence, using the same audio/music motif. No long loading freeze; hide loading behind stable transition when possible.

**Visual implementation:** animated cameras using precomputed CFrames/Cam tracks, reversible cinematic mode, volume ducking of game sounds during dialogue, streaming preload for immediate next sequence, original modeled spacecraft silhouettes, mission teleporter prepared in advance, no cinematic-specific expensive real-time AI; console/mobile optimized particle variants. Every story-critical beat has subtitles. Never show a copyright character or franchise asset. Audio may be temporary original tones with subtitles until approved voice tracks exist; explicitly list missing sound assets.

# 4. FIRST-TIME USER EXPERIENCE — OPERATION FIRST SPEAR

On first join, load account and show optional opening cinematic, then launch a **solo instanced scripted tutorial** named `OPERATION FIRST SPEAR` on a small generated-but-quality-controlled Ascendancy frontier region. Target 8–11 minutes; player always succeeds with reasonable skill. Do not send first-time players into a busy public lobby before instruction. Tutorial can later be replayed from a station training/archives screen. All combat controls, UI, support ability activations, movement, rescue rules, reward cues and extraction must be introduced through actions, not a static text page. Do not require voice audio to continue.

**Tutorial beats:**
1. **Drop (0:00–1:00):** atmospheric descent, landing pod dust and door opening, Voss instruction “First Spear, this is Command. Your target is an Ascendancy relay. Follow your HUD and move to the objective.” Prompt left stick/WASD, camera, unlimited sprint and optional jump/vault. Set initial waypoint.
2. **Traversal (1:00–2:00):** modest ledge/ridge over which player can jump/mantle; demonstrate dodge roll over a safe telegraphed training blast from distance; no sprint stamina and two rechargeable roll charges.
3. **First contact (2:00–3:30):** two basic riflemen, one scout; teach firing, aiming, reload, natural rock cover and clear enemy shield-break indication. Voss: “Ascendancy infantry ahead. Engage. Your combat shields will recover after you break contact.” In a low-danger scripted moment, demonstrate slower health regeneration beneath shields.
4. **Recover salvage (3:30–4:00):** fallen troop yields an Energy Cell; nearby secured crate contains Scrap; walk into common loot and interact with rare technology. Voss: “Recover the enemy cells. Command can repurpose their technology—but only what your squad brings home is secured.” Show shared squad salvage panel even for solo.
5. **Two abilities (4:00–5:45):** assault position with clustered infantry, tell player to select/aim/confirm Basic Airstrike; later incoming volleys prompt a temporary Dome Shield. Demonstrate cooldown timers, not limited-charge inventory, no directional Stratagem puzzle. Voss: “Support systems authorized. Mark the strike zone.” and “Deploy your barrier. Recover your shields and press forward.”
6. **Primary objective (5:45–7:30):** relay compound with a few enemies, one interactable sabotage terminal, short 15-second hold/defend, then relay explosion. No timed puzzle. Completing the relay awards secured Credits/XP and campaign progress; extraction becomes available. Voss: “Relay disabled. Primary objective complete. Extraction authorization granted.”
7. **Optional choice (7:30–8:30):** reveal a nearby crashed human sensor probe and a low-pressure elite enemy holding salvage; make this truly optional, with objective indicator distinguishing optional from primary. Voss: “Additional technology signatures detected. Investigation is your decision.”
8. **Extract (8:30–10:30):** solo player calls extraction from contextual beacon/selection; 45–75-second final defense with manageable waves; vehicle lands and boarding prompt triggers immediate win. Voss: “Final approach. Hold the landing zone.” then “First Spear complete. Welcome to the counteroffensive.”
9. **Debrief and station:** professional after-action screen showing earned Credits, XP, secure salvage, personal rank, War Contribution and next unlock; then teleport to orbital lobby. Provide basic starter gear and one standard Augment choice, but do not overload UI. Unlock public Quick Deploy, War Table, Priority Dispatch, solo/co-op operations. Easy and Normal available. Show a 15-second nonblocking spotlight/tutorial tooltip on war table and Armory.

Tutorial protection: solo automatic redeployment after death if tickets remain; no softlock; if controls or AI fail, safe fallback objective timer; repeated disconnect resumes appropriately or allows restart with credited completed rewards once. All first-time/onboarding flags saved server-side. Story can be skipped but onboarding controls must still be understandable.

# 5. CORE GAME SESSION AND PLAYER EXPERIENCE

**Moment-to-moment:** 1–4 Galactic Force soldiers select a mission at the station War Table, public join through Quick Deploy, friend invite, or manual Priority Dispatch. They deploy into a unique region, fight Ascendancy patrols, visit/ignore points of interest, complete **one core primary objective**, receive safe Credits/XP, optionally scavenge extra materials/engage elites or field bosses, call the extraction craft through squad consensus, hold the landing zone and escape. A successful extraction by at least one active eligible player secures **the full squad's shared salvage for every qualifying participant**. No compulsory second main objective and no forced survival/crafting loop.

**Mission duration:** Short ~10–12 min; Standard ~15–18; Extended ~20–25; normal post-objective exploration optional. Hard/Extreme not intrinsically longer. Soft time limit: at its expiration, Command announces support withdrawal, disables redeployment and orbital support calls (but leaves already deployed gear functioning for its remaining lifetime), and sends one final high-risk extraction even if primary objective incomplete; ~2–3 min to board. Credits/XP previously secured through completed objective stages remain. Lost/uncompleted primary objective gets no completion payout. Failed extraction forfeits unextracted salvage only.

**Extraction conditions:** no standard extraction before primary objective. When it completes, any player may propose extraction; squad vote uses a strict majority of active eligible squad members (1/1, 2/2, 2/3, 3/4), with 20-second voting window and clear controller/touch UI. An AFK player inactive for ~90 seconds does not block the vote; if near the soft limit, Command automatically initiates extraction to avoid grief. After vote, choose a valid marked reachable landing zone, communicate arrival ETA and enemy response; extraction vehicle is visually compelling and interactable, not a stationary UI teleport. Countdown after first boarding must allow a reasonable grace period for teammates and prevent grief. No players can start or join a new mission after extraction has actually started. One survivor enough to secure shared loot. If all squad members die with no redeployment tickets, mission fails.

**Gameplay rules:** no player-versus-player combat, no friendly-fire player damage at launch, no loot stealing, no inventory burden while sprinting, no arbitrary forced wait between missions, no requirement for perfectly balanced squad composition. Class and gear choices must not create mission softlocks. Voice/radio callouts are concise and tied to meaningful state changes.

**Dynamic operations:** mission type remains recognizable; procedural variability comes from world shape, optional discoveries, enemy positions, approach routes, event modifiers and weather. One primary objective, usually 0–2 optional discoveries visible, hidden rare cache chance, one optional elite/field-boss event depending on difficulty. Avoid chains that double mission duration. Generate deterministically from seed for reproducible QA. Not every mission has to contain a boss.

**Initial mission templates:**
- `SABOTAGE_RELAY`: traverse to Ascendancy relay, clear defenders, disable energy shield or terminal, place demolition charges (single interact), defend briefly, destroy structure. Alternatives: hilltop relay, canyon station, forest ridge; optional side objectives can weaken defenders but are never mandatory.
- `RECOVER_TECH`: locate human downed scientific transport/black box, fight or evade Ascendancy recovery squad, perform short scan/interact, retrieve intel and secure the mission objective; extract optional material salvage. No tedious multi-item inventory hauling.
- `DEFEND_BEACON`: secure a functioning human communication beacon, activate it, hold 2–3 controlled timed waves while repair/scan completes; optional turret cache and extra field-boss encounter.
- Expansion, not initial must-have: Escort autonomous armored logistics vehicle (NOT an underground drill), elimination of officer, recon sweep, multi-structure operation, convoy ambush, more biomes.

**Squad size scaling:** enemy health and base damage largely constant across 1–4, but groups, active enemy budgets, flank frequency and reinforcement composition scale with player count and difficulty. Boss patterns scale number of simultaneous targeted zones, not an absurd 4x HP multiplier. Solo gets reasonable reinforcement allowance and solo-compatible interactions, with no mandatory synchronized switches. All players get full personal mission payout; rewards are never divided by headcount.

# 6. WORLD GENERATION, PLANETS, MAPS, AND ART

**Launch world:** `Veyra-7`, Ascendancy-held rocky highland/frontier world with dry valleys, wind-sculpted cliffs, scattered blue-green vegetation, dusty rock flats, and abandoned human installations. Palette: cool slate terrain, warm weathered soil, cyan alien flora accents, bronze/amber Ascendancy equipment lights. Additional war-map planet names `Khepri-9` (forest frontier) and `Orion-4` (arid canyon world) may appear as future/locked theaters; do NOT falsely present unbuilt planets as playable.

**World appearance:** intentionally angular and low-poly, but not cubic voxel Minecraft terrain and not pixel art. Practical implementation: reproducible layered-noise heightfield using Roblox's native Terrain as the collision/streamable base; quantized/terraced elevation and carefully placed modular low-poly cliff/rock meshes to give distinctive faceted silhouettes; modest density of stylized flora and modular outposts. If native Terrain cannot produce a suitable angular aesthetic, test visually against low-poly chunk mesh alternatives and select only after benchmarking. Do NOT instantiate one Part per terrain voxel or make runtime EditableMesh editing a launch dependency. Native voxel terrain may still appear smooth; compensate with faceted cliff meshes and stylized shading. Use coherent material reuse and near/far asset simplification. Selected destructible rock barriers and caches can be `Model` objects with break/damage states; no fully destructible planet or required cave mining at launch.

**Initial map:** configurable roughly 1,000–1,200 studs across per side (subject to traversal/performance tests), bounded. The map must FEEL large through sightlines, generated ridges, vistas and fog; not require ten minutes of walking. One main objective normally ~300–650 traversable studs from landing, 4–7 meaningful POIs in a standard mission, at least 2 approach routes, traversable slopes and no inaccessible spawn locations. Map extent can vary by operation duration. Distant decorative peaks can extend beyond playable area, with honest boundary feedback rather than invisible surprise kills. Keep functional combat density focused, do not fill empty kilometers with repeated clutter.

**Seeded generation pipeline (server owns seed and gameplay topology):**
1. Choose planet, biome profile, operation template, difficulty, mission duration category, event modifiers and 32-bit numerical seed.
2. Compute low-frequency terrain elevation, mid-frequency variation, ridges/canyons and traversable corridors with deterministic math; reserve landing/primary/extraction/POI zones and a safe traversal spine BEFORE committing terrain.
3. Flatten/shape mission-critical footprints, check accessible grade, terrain clearance, safe spawn and at least one connected route between landing, primary and future extraction sites.
4. Build terrain in bounded chunks/tiles, applying supported Roblox APIs, scheduler budgets and optional parallelizable preparation; avoid giant single-frame blocking writes.
5. Populate scenery with seeded biome scatter, spatial exclusion rules, slope thresholds, overlap/collision tests and pooled assets; deterministic tags/attributes for POIs and enemy spawns.
6. Instantiate modules for primary objective from approved blueprint families: relay, wreck, beacon/fortified area; set interaction points, pathing/cover nodes, spawn zones, critical streamed models.
7. Add 0–2 visible optional discoveries, a limited number of hidden loot POIs, 0–1 rare dynamic events based on weights, and accessible extraction candidates.
8. Run a generator validation pass: no objectives buried, no structures floating, no blocked interactions, no excessive slopes, no disconnected routes, no extraction landing in obstacles, no unsafe spawn enemies and no resource inside terrain.
9. Publish seed and compact mission metadata to clients; clients draw map and visual dressing, but server retains authoritative mission state and validates interactions.
10. During gameplay use `StreamingEnabled`, appropriate model streaming modes, a client-facing prefetch/streaming loading state, and locally simplified distant scenery. If streaming drops a crucial objective, retain objective server state and properly prefetch/persist its model for nearby players.

**Tactical map:** complete topographic map from the start, with primary marker, major known outposts, roads/canyons, marked POIs, rough enemy-controlled regions, squad positions, synchronized waypoints/pings, and extraction candidates when authorized. Hidden salvage caches, military intel and some encounters are deliberately undisclosed. Do not force fog-of-war as the default. UI works on gamepad, touch and mouse; no tiny icons requiring pixel-perfect clicks.

**Biomes at launch:** one excellent highland biome with 2–3 subterrain palettes; expansion biomes are forests, volcanic flats, frozen valleys, deserts. Generation parameters and landmark modules are data-driven to add future planets without rewriting the system.

**Lighting/performance style:** atmospheric time-of-day, fog and color grading, sparse non-cast-shadow small props, baked-looking material variation, pooled small effects, controlled dynamic lights, low-poly clean geometric world, readable projectile VFX, sound layering. Provide `Low`, `Balanced`, `High` visual tiers (respect player's Roblox quality selection) and separate `Reduced Effects`/`Reduced Motion`. Gameplay indicators never depend on optional particles. All effects should be visually authored for this game, not copied from existing franchises.

# 7. SOLDIER MOVEMENT, CAMERA, AND SHOOTING

**Perspective:** third-person over-the-shoulder at all times (no compulsory first-person). Configurable right/left shoulder offset; aim zoom reduces camera distance; crosshair compensates for shoulder parallax. Collision-aware camera avoids walls. Reticle shows target validity and ability area. Smooth damped camera without heavy inertia, shake toggle and reduced-motion respect. For controller/touch, moderate friction-free aim assist on hostile targets only, no snap targeting or competitive advantage. PC mouse input should remain direct.

**Movement:** grounded arcade-tactical, fast and responsive. Initial tunables: walk ~17–18 studs/s, unlimited sprint ~24 studs/s, ADS ~12–14 studs/s, jump/vault when low obstacle clear, no sprint stamina. Two dodge-roll charges; roll lasts ~0.55 s and moves ~14–17 studs along intended direction; individual charges recharge ~3.5 s; allow one recovery window to avoid spam; camera remains readable. No infinite invulnerability; brief telegraph-based evasion/damage mitigation can be tuned only after testing; roll cannot pass through walls or vault off cliffs by exploit. Auto-mantle low obstacles when appropriate; crouch reduces silhouette/noise, no cover snapping, no sliding/prone at launch. Firing from sprint transitions into normal firing naturally. Use animation blending for idle/walk/sprint/strafe/aim/fire/reload/roll/vault/downed/revive/interact.

**Damage model:** 100 energy shield + 100 underlying health baseline. Shield absorbs damage first, breaks with distinctive visual/audio; after 4 s of no damage it regenerates (initial candidate 25 shield points/s). Underlying health is damaged after shield depletion; after 9 s without damage it recovers slowly (initial candidate ~8 health points/s). BOTH fully regenerate, no necessary medkits. New damage resets appropriate delays; health recovery cannot trigger while actively taking hits. Damage feedback directional and visible even with reduced effects. Downed rather than immediate death when appropriate; revive interaction baseline ~3.5 seconds; downed timer tunable per difficulty. Revive cancel if player moves/takes interruption; revive has clear UI. A solo player who is fully killed automatically uses a ticket to redeploy if available and is not required to wait for nonexistent teammates. In a four-player mission, teammates may revive downed units; fully eliminated players can redeploy using shared tickets. No forced healer class.

**Reinforcement ticket prototype per difficulty:** define player-count-sensitive budgets: Easy `3 + 2*P`, Normal `2 + 2*P`, Hard `1 + P`, Extreme `P` where `P` is active squad size 1..4; live scaling adds/removes *future* tickets conservatively, never wipes remaining tickets from the team. These yield reasonable solo viability and squad recovery; tune via telemetry. Redeployment uses a visible drop pod at a safe valid location near squad, validates collision and enemy safe zone, has protection against spawn trapping. At time-zero final emergency extraction, disable further redeployment. No exploit through repeated disconnect/rejoin.

**Gunplay:** premium weapon feel over numbers: responsive first-frame trigger, clean third-person muzzle transforms, client-local muzzle flash/hit marker/audio immediately, server-authoritative hit detection/damage, controlled recoil and bloom, impactful hit sounds, enemy hit reactions, visible enemy energy shields and armor. Support semiauto/full auto and reloads, target falloff, penetration classes for armor and shields. DO NOT have client-supplied hit target/damage accepted without server validation. Aim from reticle camera ray, but solve muzzle obstruction and nearby-wall collision to prevent shots through cover. Enemies need distinct silhouettes and high-contrast glowing shots/charge telegraphs. Friendly fire OFF at launch. Avoid excessive ballistic simulation when simple validated hitscan or low-rate physical projectiles suffice.

**Weapon stats, starting prototypes (tunable):**
- `AR-01 Service Rifle`: Rank 1 free; automatic, accurate 28-round magazine, ~600 RPM, baseline all-purpose infantry killer at every difficulty; no mandatory upgrade.
- `P-01 Service Pistol`: Rank 1 free, 12 rounds, quick switch, defensive sidearm.
- `SG-04 Breacher`: Rank 2, ~500 Credits, close-range shotgun, excellent at pushing enemy shield lines, range-limited.
- `MR-07 Ranger`: Rank 4, ~850 Credits, slower precision semi-auto rifle, weakpoint/recon focus.
- `LMG-12 Suppressor`: Rank 7, ~1,400 Credits, 75-round sustained-fire rifle with slower movement/ADS/reload.
- `ER-16 Pulse Rifle`: Rank 15, ~3,000 Credits, experimental human energy rifle with heat management and shield specialization, not universal best-in-slot.
- Later weapons: specialized anti-armor, plasma, arc/rail weapons; high-tier options should introduce trade-offs and visually remarkable behavior, not huge universal damage boosts.

**Equipment slots:** Primary firearm; secondary sidearm; throwable/grenade; two independent special ability slots; armor suit; selected class/passives. Weapon categories are not class locked. Grenades baseline fragment and later EMP; grenades are consumable within mission with modest replenishment from environmental supply caches (do not add grenade crafting). No primary heavy-weapon third active weapon slot at launch, no giant inventory UI, no compulsory first-person scope.

# 8. CLASSES AND TEAM PASSIVES (WEAPONS UNRESTRICTED)

Four **soft class presets** that define ONE personal passive and ONE squad passive; classes do NOT determine gun access or mandatory equipment. All unlocked weapons, grenades and special abilities can be mixed freely; preset loadouts are suggestions editable by the user. Every class is available early and changeable at station between deployments. Same-class duplicate team effects do not stack; the strongest same-type aura applies once. Squad-wide passives always affect solo players. If personal and team effects affect the same property on the same player, use safe bounded additive/multiplicative stacking and show resulting stats explicitly.

- **Assault:** Personal reload speed +10%; team reload speed +5%. Suggest AR, airstrike, sentry as nonbinding example. Role: flexible frontline.
- **Heavy:** Personal max shield +15%; team max shield +5%. Suggest LMG, dome, orbital as nonbinding example. Role: absorbing focused fire, holding ground.
- **Recon:** Personal dodge charge recharge speed +15%; team movement speed +5%. Suggest marksman, scanner (later), airstrike. Role: recon and mobility; tactical map remains universally available, Recon is never required.
- **Support:** Personal revive speed +20%; team underlying health regeneration speed +10%. Suggest versatile carbine, shield, turret. Role: sustain and recovery, not mandatory healer.

These percentages are balancing seeds. For solo and squad, keep max effective modifiers capped (e.g., reload or movement <=20% total) to avoid speed exploits and make team compositions optional. No character-level talent trees, skill branches or class-exclusive guns. Show class passive effects clearly in Armory and squad UI.

# 9. SPECIAL ABILITIES — DIRECT, COOLDOWN-BASED

Every soldier can equip EXACTLY **two special abilities** independent of class. Both have automatic cooldown regeneration, unlimited activations per mission while Command support is operational. No stratagem arrow-code puzzles, no per-mission charge economy, no special battery currency needed to activate. Use select → place/aim → confirm, cancellable before confirmation. Show ground area preview, cast arrival warning, friendly-safe VFX, cooldown progress, and accessibility/reduced effects alternatives. Client predicts markers and visuals, server validates readiness, valid zone, ally/mission restrictions, cooldown and target before applying effects.

**Standard abilities:**
1. `Tactical Airstrike`: available Rank 1; ~90s cooldown; aircraft flyover + linear bomb pattern effective against groups of Ascendancy infantry. Visible warning for all allies; no friendly-fire damage. Moderate vs heavy boss armor.
2. `Dome Shield`: available Rank 1; ~70s cooldown; placeable temporary protective energy bubble, approximately 8–10s duration and limited damage absorption; stops enemy projectiles, not invincible. Maximum one active personal dome.
3. `Automated Sentry`: Rank 4, purchase with Credits (~900 initial); ~100s cooldown; temporary stationary turret with bounded target detection, line-of-sight checks, limited ammo/duration, maximum one active personal turret; server owns targeting/damage. Turret cannot farm idle enemies indefinitely.
4. `Orbital Precision Strike`: Rank 8, purchase with Credits (~1,800 initial); ~150s cooldown; visually spectacular focused strike against armored units, installations and boss armor; telegraph time and line-of-fire checks; cannot instantly trivialize major boss phases.

**Experimental abilities:** earned through certain major boss blueprint unlocks (rare drop plus guaranteed research progress). Example `Orbital Lance`: narrow sustained guided beam, anti-armor precision, poor crowd clear, longer cooldown. Example `Reflective Barrier`: short smaller dome reflecting limited energy rounds, vulnerable to explosives. They are situational alternatives, not superior copies; a Rank 1 Airstrike stays valuable throughout the game.

**Ability Augments:** one separate equipped special-ability Augment per soldier at launch, applied to either of the two abilities; e.g., Focused Airstrike (narrower pattern, higher armor damage), Extended Turret Magazine (longer fire, longer cooldown), Reinforced Dome (more shield durability, shorter duration). Do not introduce additional Augment slots or generic power trees until core balance validated. Cooldowns configurable per ability and can be tuned globally. If time limit passes, previously selected orbital support becomes unavailable as Command withdraws; no reset/cooldown workaround.

**Performance:** Server owns battlefield effects. Client renders pooled particle/beam assets; use inexpensive area-attack simulation and low-cost single projectiles rather than hundreds of unanchored parts. Concurrent audio is ducked/mixed; in eight-player future raids enforce tighter VFX budgets. Prioritize readable damage zones, enemy silhouettes and shield breaks over particle density.

# 10. AUGMENTS, WEAPON MASTERY, AND PROGRESSION DESIGN

Augments modify behavior, not a random rarity-based power ladder. Start weapons with **one Core Augment slot**. Every individual weapon has a separate **five-level Weapon Mastery** bar; at Mastery 5 its **second Auxiliary Augment slot** unlocks permanently for that weapon. Target about 3–5 standard missions of real use, calibrated with objective participation, damage and assists rather than kill-stealing. No paid skip. Auxiliary slot can equip only utility/handling Augments (no second large damage multiplier); keep starting players competitive. No individual per-Augment leveling, random numerical rolls, combining duplicates, disposable rerolls or mastery reset on death.

**Augment compatibility:** Mix universal Augments (across compatible firearms), category-level Augments (all compatible assault rifles, shotguns, marksman, machine guns, energy weapons) and special-ability-specific Augments. Once researched and owned, a blueprint is permanent and can be equipped freely across all compatible guns; mastery slot availability is individual gun-specific. Never require re-crafting the same Augment for each gun. Present compatibility and pros/cons in the UI.

**Initial Augment catalog, concise and purposeful:**
- `Stability Compensator` (universal core): -15% recoil, -8% ADS speed.
- `Quick Feed` (universal core): +12% reload speed, -10% magazine size.
- `Extended Magazine` (compatible core): +20% magazine size, +12% reload time.
- `Shield Disruptor` (category core): bonus vs energy shields, lower health damage.
- `Precision Chamber` (marksman/AR core): bonus weakpoint reliability, slower follow-up shots.
- `Quick Draw` (universal auxiliary): faster weapon swap, no damage bonus.
- `Target Discipline` (universal auxiliary): faster recovery after ADS, no damage bonus.
- `Reinforced Grip` (compatible auxiliary): improved handling while moving, no damage bonus.
- `Extended Sentry Magazine` (ability-specific): more turret ammo, longer cooldown.
- `Focused Orbital Strike` (boss experimental ability Augment): smaller blast radius, better anti-armor effect.
These are balance prototypes; first playable vertical slice can implement 4 standard + 1 boss Augment, later initial release ~8–10 meaningful modifiers.

**Economy:**
- **Military XP / Rank** (account progression): access to weapon/ability purchases, Hard/Extreme, boss clearances. Ranks ~1–30 initially; extend to 50+ without changing player-power curve.
- **Credits (Cr)**: currency earned by mission objectives, optional objectives, and commendations. Buy new unlocked weapons, ability licenses, standard Augment research and cosmetics. Prices sane; early alternate weapon obtainable after ~2–3 successful missions; most standard gear accessible within ~15–25 hours of normal play.
- **Scrap:** common recovered material, basic Augment crafting/selling.
- **Energy Cells:** recovered from Ascendancy energy equipment and elites; used for energy-related research/ability Augments.
- **Military Technology:** rarer recovered material from facilities/commanders and bosses; advanced research, no trading or paid loot boxes.
- **Weapon Mastery:** non-spendable per-gun use progression. **Boss Research**: persistent blueprint counters, not a new currency. **Galactic War Contribution**: non-spendable community campaign progress. **Service Commendations**: repeatable achievement rewards, not an inflationary currency.

**Example payouts for a completed ~15-min Normal operation:** 450 Credits and 600 XP safe on objective completion, plus around 12 Scrap, 4 Energy Cells, 1 Military Technology *if successfully scavenged and extracted*. Payout candidates Easy ~250Cr/330XP; Hard ~700Cr/950XP; Extreme ~950Cr/1350XP; scale loot table rarity with difficulty, not access to every essential material. Difficulty multipliers, per-hour economics, mission durations and shop prices must be configured and tested. No guaranteed 100% loot every time, but progression must be predictable overall. Solo receives full rewards; team members get full shared salvage, not a quarter share.

**First-time unlock path:** Rank 1 AR + pistol + grenade + Airstrike + Dome, one Augment slot, all four soft classes; Rank 2 shotgun; Rank 4 marksman and sentry; Rank 6 Hard difficulty; Rank 7 LMG; Rank 8 Orbital Strike; Rank 10 first major Ascendancy boss clearance; Rank 14 Extreme; Rank 15 energy rifle; subsequent ranks unlock additional equipment/cosmetics/operations. Rank thresholds should be tuned so a player generally has the Credits necessary for a new rank's core purchase within 1–3 missions. Every standard weapon remains viable at Extreme against ordinary enemies.

**Boss blueprint protection:** rare experimental drop chance initial candidate 25% per qualifying clear; if no drop, guaranteed blueprint on third qualifying extraction. First-time blueprint ownership persists; duplicate drops convert to appropriate salvage/research credit, never junk. A `qualifying clear` requires meaningful participation, defeating the boss and a successful team extraction; one surviving squad member secures the shared boss salvage. No paid random loot boxes or loot-based pressure tactics. Dedicated repeatable boss operations remain accessible once unlocked, even if community campaign status changes.

**Shared salvage rules:** Squad-level mission inventory holds collected Scrap, Energy Cells and Military Technology, displayed unobtrusively. Common scrap is auto-collected near the squad, rare caches require interaction, all contributions aggregate once. On extraction success with >=1 eligible survivor, server grants each qualifying squad member **the full shared quantities**; do not divide by player count or give only the looter the rare item. On total team extraction failure, the squad loses unsecured salvage, but keeps objective-secured Credits/XP and war contribution. Dying/redeploying doesn't delete shared salvage. Joining late grants eligible rewards only if real participation is demonstrated (time in operation, damage/assists, objectives, defending or rescues); handle support roles fairly. Server tracks contribution. Never trust a client-sent inventory quantity. Cap drop generation and pooling to avoid littering the world with hundreds of physical objects.

**Progression pacing:** ~0–2h onboarding, first purchases and augment; 2–8h more guns/abilities and experimentation; 8–15h boss and advanced unlocks; 15–25h majority of standard arsenal; 25h+ rare technology, mastery across guns, cosmetic achievements and evolving Galactic War. No artificial FOMO or daily login requirements. No weapon rarity tiers that make the AR-01 useless. Track median time to first meaningful unlock, total Credit inflation, player ability usage and failure rates.

# 11. THE ASCENDANCY — ENEMY AI AND ENCOUNTER DESIGN

**Faction personality:** disciplined hostile alien military, unlike shambling hordes or mechanized-only robot factions. Enemies communicate local detections, defend positions, establish suppressive lanes, flank occasionally and respond to destroyed communications. At higher difficulties, more units/combined archetypes/elite support, not invulnerable bullet sponges or omniscient shots. Readable energy projectiles and telegraphed heavy attacks give dodge meaningful use.

**Launch enemy roster:**
1. `Ascendancy Rifleman` (standard): 1x silhouette, mid-range plasma carbine burst, uses rocks/barricades, guards locations, backs off when outnumbered. Small Scrap and occasional Energy Cell drop.
2. `Ascendancy Scout` (standard): lighter armor, fast lateral motion, close-range bursts, may activate reinforcement beacon if unchallenged; visible rising transmission icon and cancel opportunity. Scarcer drops.
3. `Ascendancy Marksman` (specialist): long-range charge rifle with visible targeting beam and ≥1s warning; repositioning and dodge counterplay; does not land impossible instant hits through cover.
4. `Ascendancy Heavy` (elite/miniboss-tier): taller armor/energy mantle, charged area-burst or explosive projectile and exposed core/weak point; limited frequency on Easy/Normal, common tactical threat on Hard/Extreme. Guaranteed Energy Cell chance and occasional Military Technology.
5. `Ascendancy Officer` (elite): commands a small squad, gives nearby units improved coordination/reinforcement call and has a unique energy-sweep attack with clear telegraph. A valuable target in optional elimination events; rare technology chance.
6. `Bastion Walker` (field boss): imposing bipedal alien armored walker with identifiable leg actuators/shoulder artillery and a weak core. Roaming/triggered rarely, not always tied to a dedicated arena. Attacks: directed cannon sweep, slow bombardment telegraph, infantry call. Destroy components to change behavior, but do not simulate 50 physics pieces. Valuable salvage, defeatable with regular weapons by hitting weak points, faster with proper special abilities.
7. `High Marshal Veyr` (major boss): named powerful Ascendancy commander with distinctive large armor and command aura. Dedicated Rank 10 operation; short approach + command installation optional defenses + two readable combat phases; phases change patterns, not a 10x HP inflation. Phase 1: shield pulse/targeted rifle volleys/controlled infantry; Phase 2: mobility burst and artillery target zones/exposed core. Anti-cheese boundaries without invisible walls. Boss assault produces a unique blueprint chance with 3-clear guarantee.

**AI architecture:** server authoritative finite-state machines/behavior modules `Idle`, `Patrol`, `Suspicious`, `Investigate`, `TakeCover`, `Advance`, `Suppress`, `Flank`, `Attack`, `Retreat`, `CallReinforcements`, `Stunned`, `Dead`. Central encounter director allocates an active enemy budget and staggered decision ticks based on relevance/distance; not every enemy does expensive pathfinding every heartbeat. Precompute usable cover anchors and navigation links in objective/outpost modules. Use line-of-sight raycasts, path recompute throttles, path fail fallback, pooled VFX, movement sanity, threat decay when no contact. Enemies should see/communicate realistically, not shoot through terrain or target unloaded players. AI tactical sophistication arises from limited coordinated roles rather than deep reinforcement learning.

**Threat levels:** `Unaware`, `Investigating`, `Mobilizing`, `Lockdown`. Triggers: being sighted and allowing scout transmit, attacks on outposts, alarms, time in territory. Destroying an Ascendancy relay can reduce reinforcements even if the baseline regional heat remains high. Reduce idle enemy spawning; no endlessly escalating waves solely to farm Credits. Direct kill XP should be minor vs objective completion to avoid exploit farming. Persist alert only within mission, except select campaign modifiers.

**Encounter budgets (starting, benchmark required):** cap ~10–12 actively thinking nearby NPCs for Normal four-player instances, ~14–18 for Hard/Extreme if actual Xbox One measurements permit; solo generally fewer. Prefer staged reinforcements replacing defeated units to rendering 100 at once. Active projectiles are bounded; cleanup on death/despawn. Larger “hordes” at Hard/Extreme achieved through paced groups and coordinated flanks, not constant overpopulation. Distinct elite/miniboss frequency scales with difficulty and operation intensity; no forced elite every Easy mission.

**Difficulty presets:**
| Setting | Initial access | Threat design | Relative completion rewards |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | After tutorial | Smaller patrols, slow reaction, forgiving revive/extraction, scarce elites | 1.0x |
| Normal | After tutorial | Baseline disciplined squads, occasional elites, standard attack | ~1.7x Easy |
| Hard | Rank 6 | More coordinated squads, scouts, heavies, field-boss chance | ~2.6x Easy |
| Extreme | Rank 14 | More combined-arms events, flank pressure, frequent elites and hardest extraction, never cheating shots | ~3.5x Easy |

Explicitly no excessively raised enemy HP by difficulty; increases in spawn composition, intelligence parameters, reaction windows and special encounters are more important. Boss HP may scale modestly by squad size with caps. All reward multipliers and enemy counts data-driven, playtested. Joining a mission increases difficulty for **future** encounter waves gradually, not an instant spike.

Teleport into same running mission instance via supported current APIs. Spawn arriving player at a safe allied drop point; show mission state from server; share live squad salvage and squad passive updates. Reconnect same player after brief disconnect if session is active and slot available, restoring account inventory but avoiding duplicate rewards/tickets. Save selectable loadout presets locally on account (server-authoritative persistence) and use an armory preview model with correct cosmetics. Avoid dozens of nested screens.

**HUD:** top-left compact shield/health bars, downed/revive indication; reticle/ADS center; ammo and reload near right; bottom Ability 1/2 icons + cooldown radial/number; two roll charges; grenades; unobtrusive squad status and reinforcement tickets; primary objective progress and soft timer; compass/waypoint, contextual ping; small 3-item squad salvage readout optionally expandable. Reduce clutter on mobile by concise icons and safe-area layout. War table/maps and menus readable at console TV distance. Show current controls based on active input device.

# 17. PERFORMANCE, STREAMING, AND DEVICE-SPECIFIC TARGETS

**Baseline target:** original Xbox One at a *measured, stable 30 FPS* in representative four-player Normal/Hard combat, with no major simulation stalls; higher-quality devices should aim for 60 FPS without requiring them. Device targets are not guarantees before physical profiling. Include low-end Android/iOS representative devices; ensure graphics scaled to their capabilities. Use Device Emulator for visual/layout checks but not as proof of memory/performance. Test on real hardware or label platform unverified.

**Performance discipline:** enable appropriate Workspace streaming, tagged/per-instance/level-of-detail models, short draw distances in crowded combat, fog and far vista mesh proxies; avoid huge replication payloads in `ReplicatedStorage`; defer nonessential world decoration until after player can move; generation in bounded work batches with client loading UI. Do not generate a full 2,000-stud superdense world by default or place millions of Parts. Pool projectiles and FX; no per-shot physical debris. Keep AI/server pathfinding on budget with near/far update loops; avoid per-frame global workspace scans. Server decides damage and game state; expensive visual details local-only. Use original compressed textures with shared atlases/material variants where possible, minimal transparency overdraw, LOD for troops/world props, high-impact low-cost clouds/skyboxes, and restrained shadow casters.

**Adaptive tiers:** `Low` — shorter foliage range, reduced shadows, fewer particles, simplified weather, reduced animation embellishments, same gameplay telegraphs and geometry; `Balanced` — default low-end console visual target; `High` — more atmosphere and effects on capable PC/newer devices. Never reduce collision or objective availability by tier. Do not tie mechanics to client graphics quality. Store player preference, but respect Roblox automatic graphics level.

**Suggested early budgets (not hard engine facts):** four active players; ~10–12 Normal combat NPCs, ~14–18 Hard/Extreme if profiling allows; no more than one active personal turret and one dome per soldier; ≤2–3 simultaneous visually dominant strikes near camera, queued/visually simplified if more; only 4–7 POIs on typical map; modest animated vegetation; hub 12–16 characters. Every number is tunable after measuring real CPU/GPU/network/memory. Prioritize enemy AI, responsiveness, UI and extraction over decoration.

**Monitoring:** capture client FPS/1% lows, frame time spikes, memory, script activity, network send/receive, server frame/heartbeat, AI path requests and active actor counts in instrumentation dashboards. Test at worst-case 4 players, all ability slots active, 2 elites/field boss, extraction enemy pressure and particle effects. Track mission generation duration separately. Run profiling at every milestone and fix major regressions before adding features. Memory leaks in repeated mission cycles are launch blockers; verify cleanup of connections, temporary tags, models, FX, mission state and UI.

# 23. THE ORBITAL STATION: LITERAL INTERIOR, WINDOWS, SPACE VISTAS, SOUND, AND NAVIGATION

**Canonical station name:** `GFS VANGUARD`, a Galactic Force orbital command carrier/station in high orbit of the current front's staging world. This is not a warehouse, gray lobby box, waiting room, empty platform, or supermarket-style shop floor. It is the accessible portion of a huge credible military ship: armored interior, observation concourse, logistics bay, data-equipped command deck, service corridors, human operations personnel, active defense and flight operations. Players visibly occupy a real starship hundreds of meters above a planet. Scale and framing communicate enormous unseen ship volume without rendering the whole vessel. It is not free-roam ship piloting.

**Fixed visual anchor—the observation windows:** the primary station concourse MUST have a long, unmistakable row of tall, angled, reinforced panoramic windows facing space. Minimum three large visually contiguous window bays with visible structural mullions and optional subtle holographic protective glazing. Each bay should extend well above soldier head height; show a convincing deep black-blue starfield, a large partially lit planetary limb or terminator through at least one window, scattered distant stars and occasional small fleet/transport silhouettes. This is what the player sees within the first **five seconds** of arriving in the hub. The window scene cannot be a static black void or a flat star-image pasted directly on a wall. A high-quality planet render/skybox or layered distant geometry with gentle relative motion is acceptable; do not make stars race at absurd speed. When an active front changes, station exterior vista can update via themed planet/distant space graphics, but never imply the station teleported in real time without narrative justification. The ship rotates or distant ships drift subtly at a slow believable rate; never disorient players or cause motion sickness. Windows must be solid with collision; players cannot fall into space or jump into decorative geometry.

**Views outside the windows** in a normal hub session: (a) dominant planet with correctly curved horizon, atmospheric rim and believable surface/color; (b) 5–20 distant tiny stars/sky glints and a faint atmospheric dust band, visually authored rather than repeated spherical Parts; (c) occasional small transport moving on a precomputed safe cinematic spline once every ~60–150 seconds, not 40 simultaneous physics vehicles; (d) 1–3 very distant capital-ship silhouettes if performance permits; (e) emergency red/orange navigation beacons on the hull and soft hangar guide lights. None may occlude interactable UI or tank performance. Include an observation rail at comfortable distance from glass and subtle rim highlights when standing nearby. Maintain perimeter.` Defensive reinforcement waves are *finite* and modest; objective device countdown ~30s with opportunities to shorten by disabling power node (optional). Explosion destroys spire (scenic collapse) and updates task. Side option `Disable perimeter scanner` reduces 1 scheduled wave; not required. One rare hidden vault near base may contain Military Technology. Completion unlocks extraction vote immediately. Fail if full team eliminated; timed emergency possible with partial credit for cleared sub-objectives but not full completion. Variation: cliff radar, canyon bridge uplink, ridge fortress tower, different generator orientation; preserve interact path and two approach routes.

**RECOVER_TECH concrete sequence:** briefing `RECOVER SURVEY BLACKBOX — Ascendancy teams have reached a Galactic Force wreck.` Site lies in open valley, ledge or cracked plateau. Two or three patrols search surrounding routes. Players find marked crash debris and follow short-range ping to sealed recorder cabinet. Upon reaching site, a timed scan ~12s must be initiated from one terminal (one interaction), countdown pauses if player moves out of a generous defensible area or terminal is disrupted—not reset entirely for petty damage. Enemy response briefly mobilizes to site; only 1–2 controlled squads arrive. Successful scanner animation releases small hovering data canister or readable blackbox light, *no manual escort of a fragile object*. Mission state becomes `INTEL RECOVERED`; extraction available. Optional second wreck case yields shared salvage only. Variation: enemy already guarding wreck, contested patrol approaches from ridge, sensor mine warning, occasional elite commander overseeing recovery; do not run all variants together. Audio: weak emergency locator chirp increasing as player approaches, Voss confirms recorder secured.

**DEFEND_BEACON concrete sequence:** briefing `RESTORE THE LINE — Reestablish Galactic Force communication in the sector.` Beacon site on defensible rise with clear approach directions. Player clears 2–4 on-site enemies, accesses console, holds interact ~2s, visibly restores power, dish starts turning. Three **finite** defense rounds over ~3–4 minutes on Normal, with distinct brief 15–20 second lulls to reposition/reload. Bar fills as broadcast progress instead of feeling arbitrary. Wave A: riflemen/scouts; wave B: riflemen and shield trooper, possible telegraphed flank; wave C: suppressor/heavy and supporting riflemen, with elite only on high difficulty/rare roll. This is not horde survival. Each wave has clear spawn approach/telegraph and active enemy cap; behind-the-player spawns forbidden near the defended zone. If enemies contest the beacon, timer pauses until nearby hostile threat dealt with; no infinite restart unless signal console itself briefly disabled. Player can inspect small optional supply cache or short auxiliary shield node, not multiple long side quests. On completion, transmission pulse, Voss `Beacon secured. Command has the sector. Extraction authorized.` Variation: wind valley, elevated ruins, open plateau with sniping outcrop. No 4-player simultaneous levers.

**Regular mission optional content:** 1–2 visible optional POIs per run, e.g., deserted human cache (+Scrap), enemy power crate (+Energy Cells), abandoned antenna (+intelligence reducing alarm), 0–1 hidden rare cache (+Military Technology), 0–1 optional high-risk elite/field-boss event. Too many map symbols defeat exploration; important military installations map-known from start, some salvage unknown. If player skips all optional content they still complete mission and receive core safe Credits/XP. Successful pickup never divides or steals from teammates. A rare field boss may visibly patrol or arrive when alert high; no forced encounter that converts 15-minute run into 35-minute endurance battle.

**Enemy Response Director micro rules:** finite encounter-budget tokens per mission/difficulty; add weighted reinforcement when team generates noise, triggers alarms or lingers near hostile outpost. Actual enemies spawn at believable behind-ridge / shuttle-drop points outside player direct focus and beyond minimum safety range. Alert states `Unaware`, `Investigating`, `Mobilizing`, `Lockdown` must give readable warning: radio static, enemy shouts/in-world indicator, Voss radio line once, icon on HUD. Killing a scout before radio transmission prevents that alarm's reinforcements. Defeating a wave decreases active pressure temporarily; do not make enemy reinforcements endless or respawn identically every 30 seconds. Director may respond faster on Extreme but still obey active actor caps and line-of-sight restrictions. Do not scale single rifleman HP by 4x for four players; increase coordinated enemy formation complexity.

**Soft timer micro rules:** display remaining support window, not `mission instantly fails in N`. At 5 min warn lightly; 2 min urgent warning; at 0 Voss `Command support is withdrawing. Final emergency extraction inbound. Last window.` Disable NEW support ability activations, stop ticket redeploys, choose the safest available final extraction pad; arrival and boarding stay functional. Any previously completed primary remains completed. If primary uncompleted at zero, emergency extraction can still rescue squad/salvage, but no full objective payout. Do not reset every cooldown exactly at timer expiration or cancel existing turret/dome violently. Ensure stuck players are not trapped behind new objective geometry during emergency.

**Mission seed reuse:** repeated same seed+template+difficulty should reproduce macro map/POI topology for QA; random order and mission selection use independent deterministic generators. Cosmetic background particles may be nondeterministic since they have no gameplay authority. If reused seed with changed configuration version creates different result, include generator version in bug report. Store `planetID`, `templateID`, `seed`, `difficulty`, `durationCategory`, `generatorVersion`, `missionServerJobID`, `squadID` in session metadata.

# 30. MICRO-COMBAT FIDELITY: EXACT INPUT RESPONSE, HIT FEEL, PLAYER ANIMATION, CAMERA

This game lives or dies by shooting 95% of the time. Build with a purpose-made playable character controller and real weapon rigs before adding decorative features. Use consistent Roblox Humanoid/controller practices, server ownership for damage/mission, and local responsiveness for sight/audio feedback. Avoid custom physics so elaborate that an old console cannot sustain it.

**Third-person camera default:** rear-right over-shoulder centered approximately behind human character head/upper torso, target camera distance tunable around 7–10 studs when hip firing; ADS tightens to ~4–6 studs and shifts shoulder slightly while keeping a clear reticle and not placing gun through camera. Dynamic obstruction handling uses camera ray/geometry to zoom in near walls, but retains aim correct even when extremely close to cover. Right shoulder default, left shoulder toggle persists in settings. Camera yaw smooth for joystick/touch, mouse immediate/low lag; avoid dead-feeling inertia and giant cinematic lag. Sprint modestly widens FOV (~4 degrees) if reduced-motion off, roll camera follows player without spinning, damage shake small and directional rather than full-screen seizure. ADS near wall should lower/flag weapon if muzzle obstructed so firing cannot shoot through stone. A tight shoulder offset must not cause bullets to miss a reticle target due to camera parallax at 3–15 studs.

**Combat movement priority:** moving while firing and strafe aiming must be fluid. Normal walk 17–18 studs/s; sprint ~24, unlimited; ADS ~12–14; crouch decreases movement to a tuned lower pace and camera smoothly follows. Two roll charges visible beside ability HUD; roll is controllable directional and gives clear animation/audio with bounded recovery, does not use actual physics to clip under floors; cancel conflicting actions consistently. Reload may interrupt sprint modestly but doesn't fully freeze movement. If a player presses fire immediately after a completed weapon swap the weapon responds within animation rules. Sprint does not drain energy/shield; rolls recharge independently; grenade cooldown/ammo (inventory count) is not to be conflated with the unlimited rechargeable two special ability slots. Prior spec gives two grenades variants and normal throwable capacity; starter 2 frag grenades per mission with normal resupply if the design provides a supply crate, configurable, do not add a new currency to throw grenades. Health and shield regen preserve previous exact 4s / 9s delay logic.

**Weapon shot timeline:** input press -> immediate local trigger/audio + muzzle flash shell/bolt motion + reticle recoil -> server validates fire rate, ammo, direction, aim bounds, line of sight and hit -> authoritative hit state -> client receives confirm and hit marker; a confirmed kill has a distinct restrained audio/reticle confirmation. Client must not show confirmed kill/loot until server acknowledgment. At longer latency, client may predict harmless tracers, not damage. One visual tracer per meaningful shot/burst is sufficient, not thousands of physical bullets. Controlled impacts: dusty stone puff for terrain, tiny sparks for armor, energy ripple for shield, bright localized weakpoint flare for bosses. Close-range gunshots should feel weighty with initial transient, mechanical body, fast decay and quiet environmental tail. Never play a giant thunderclap every bullet. Audio and recoil variation make rifle memorable; standard AR-01 remains satisfying at any rank.

**Recoil and crosshair:** HIP reticle four small lines with readable center dot, expands modestly during sustained automatic fire/sprint; ADS tighter reticle and weapon sights/optic if available; targeting an interactable turns small center reticle cyan and displays on-screen contextual prompt. Hit marker white for ordinary damage, violet/amber for shield/armor if colors are paired with recognizable shape/audio, short outer flourish for weak point and confirmed kill. Slight punchy recoil climbs and recovers; first shot precise enough to reward aim; no full-screen obscuring muzzle flash. Firing while moving less accurate but viable, not random spray with no agency. ADS camera/sights should show environmental silhouettes clearly.

**Magazine, reload, and inspect details:** persistent ammo in-mag per weapon and reasonable reserve ammo within mission; no purchasable ammo currency. Reload transition: hand reaches mag -> old magazine detach -> new magazine attach -> weapon charge/bolt -> `Ready`. Animation timing and ammo award must align authoritatively with a configurable reload-complete event or secure server timer; don't let players exploit swapping equipment to refill ammo instantly. If mag empty and reserve exists, optional auto-reload begins with audio; if reserve empty, dry-fire click and clear `NO AMMO` feedback; pistol remains accessible. Visible ammo count located lower-right near gun icon. Shotgun reload may be shell-by-shell only if robust; simpler magazine animation is acceptable for initial build. Weapons have distinct hand positions and fire rates; scope doesn't appear as fake floating reticle detached from gun.

**Damage/shield state details:** on first impact show short clean shield shimmer on soldier body and small hit-direction arc; shield bar drops instantly and regen delay marker/tiny dim pulse indicates charge pending. On shield break, brief distinct crackle with ring ripple, light cyan shards (cheap particles), one quiet Voss warning at most per reasonable cooldown, health begins taking damage. Health loss uses muted red vignette only at low HP and never covers reticle entirely. Health regeneration after 9 seconds is clearly slower than shield and cannot happen during sustained damage. At health 0 go downed with intentional animation, camera lowers but remains player controlled, 3rd person and no fake blood. Teammates see downed beacon plus revive prompt when close; revive progress ring remains centered at teammate with clear interrupt behavior. Fully dead camera shows tactically useful spectator of squadmate, server-side ticket total and respawn countdown, not a frozen black screen. Solo with tickets instantly proceeds to short visible pod arrival sequence; no mandatory revive by teammates.

**Reticle and UI feedback priority:** hierarchy critical danger > objective interaction > ability targeting > weapon hit confirm > ambient prompts. Only one oversized center-screen callout at a time, e.g., `OBJECTIVE COMPLETE`, appearing ~1.5s then fading. UI should never block a roll/aim input because an unnecessary menu popped up. Camera and reticle update at render cadence locally; server networking does not control each frame of camera.

**Enemy-hit feedback:** enemy flinches for moderate hits with short animation but does not stunlock under four assault rifles; armored enemies show subtle localized sparks; heads/weakpoints are readable but not exaggerated giant red discs. Hit damage numbers disabled by default or small/restrained and can be toggled; large numbers flying across screen would undermine serious military tone. On death, body drops with grounded animation/ragdoll if optimized, weapon/cell occasionally drops, remains for a short configurable period then fades/culls, no instant pop/dissolve under ordinary conditions. Never create unanchored hundreds-of-parts ragdolls.

**First-fire benchmark:** player stands at ~40 studs from an Ascendancy rifleman among three rocks; move/ADS/fire from cover; enemy communicates and shifts position; AR muzzle, impact, hit markers, shield/health bars, knockback animations and reload all synchronize; support airstrike deletes an enemy cluster with controlled VFX and leaves terrain/players intact. Record actual gameplay footage and evaluate in motion, not just still screen.

# 31. ENEMY VISUAL/AI ROLES, TELEGRAPHS, AND FAIRNESS

The five common/higher-level humanoid launch roles are Rifleman, Scout, Marksman, Heavy and Officer (plus Bastion Walker and High Marshal Veyr). These must be visually and behaviorally distinct at a glance. Do not produce one generic mesh with only different `WalkSpeed` or arbitrary health multiples. Each role has its own stance, equipment silhouette, attack tells, movement radius, target choice and distinct sound bank. Implement tactical behaviors through bounded server state machines.

**Rifleman:** basic alien armored silhouette with midweight rifle, patterned 2–4-round plasma bursts separated by small recovery; can crouch behind lower cover, peek, advance toward cover 8–20 studs away and retreat when outnumbered. Search at last known target location if vision lost, not x-ray track through the ridge. Sound: 3-note plasma crackle, distant alien radio grunt when spots player. Dying yields light scrap and occasional cell; modest HP, killable quickly with AR when exposed. On Easy, gives generous attack warning/reaction time, not laser aim.

**Scout:** narrower fast agile profile with comms antenna and compact short plasma rifle; seeks side routes and tries to transmit alarm with visible antenna pulse/ascending 2s meter. Player can prevent transmission by killing/stunning/temporarily disrupting; if interrupted, director will not send corresponding wave. Scout can strafe but must not teleport or spin erratically. Audio signature high-pitched comm sweep and fast footfall. Distinct green-ish or violet glowing indicator but not pure neon.

**Marksman:** slim long weapon, briefly anchors a narrow warning line toward approximate target location for >1 second before firing; warning line is visible to target and dodgeable even on Extreme (increase targeting speed slightly but not impossibly); high damage but small rate of fire. Relocates after ~2 shots to nearby named positions, not outside world. Marksman can't target behind opaque terrain or shoot through cover. Distinct charge whine increasing pitch.

**Heavy:** thick armor and obvious glowing central chest plate, slower turning, broad energy projector; telegraphs wide blast zone through 1.2–1.8s windup and a ground indicator. A heavy should force rolling/cover, but not one-shot an undamaged Easy player without enormous warning. Exposed core weakpoint/rear vents allow starter rifle damage; orb strike helps but not required. Its kill has armor collapse/crackle, not a giant expensive physics explosion. Spawns rarely Easy, occasional Normal, strategically in Hard/Extreme.

**Officer:** ivory rank plates, high comms fins, deliberate command gestures, mid-range energy sweep; buffs nearby coordination modestly and occasionally orders reinforcement if an intact local relay exists. Killing Officer removes the buff and interrupts the command call; show support soldiers momentarily losing coordination. Voice style aggressive alien radio barks, untranslated yet clearly threatening. Used as leader in rare patrols and some optional events, not in every group.

**Bastion Walker:** field boss has large bipedal mechanical-armor silhouette, thick armored legs and one luminous vulnerable central power unit. Its 3 attack patterns: cannon sweep telegraphed by pivoting gun arm, delayed artillery circles on ground, short infantry reinforcement call capped at 2–4 soldiers. Aimable leg actuator parts can disable one attack or stagger walker, but preserve readable health/phase if simplification necessary. At high level it is dangerous and rewarding, but avoiding it is allowed in normal missions. Cannot path through cliffs; if navigation fails it retreats/resets position rather than teleporting onto players. Field boss should be a memorable ~2–4-minute optional fight if chosen, not a 15-minute sponge.

**High Marshal Veyr:** named major boss mission accessed at Rank 10 from the War Table and Archive. A large armored Ascendancy commander in an impressive military amphitheater/fortified command plaza generated from a curated arena template; different from Walker. Clearly masculine/feminine alien physiology need not mirror human; authority established by helmet crest, armor rank, body language and voice modulation. Two major phases: shield-command phase with guarded power pylons and aimed plasma volley; broken shield initiates transition/squad advance; second phase quicker mobile strike, ground hazard circles and vulnerable core after high-power attack. Visual telegraphs >=0.7s for major unavoidable-looking blasts even at highest difficulty, longer as needed for Xbox latency/touch. All attacks must have counterplay: move, roll, cover or stop charge by destroying exposed component; no random unavoidable wipe. Solo boss feasible with adapted simultaneous hazards, reduced assistant enemies and same readable rules. Do not impose multiple mandatory simultaneous switches. On death, pause combat pressure briefly, dramatic contained energy collapse, intact recoverable experimental blueprint reward if eligible, then clear extraction. 3-clear blueprint pity is server authoritative and persists.

**Enemy perception:** raycast to multiple plausible player body points at bounded frequency; store last seen coordinate/time, react to audible events with location uncertainty; lose exact player location behind opaque cover after a meaningful delay. A scout who saw a player can alert teammates near them; a rifleman out of comms range does not instantly learn every player location. Cover positions should have explicit exposed/hidden geometry and server validation so enemies don't stand firing repeatedly into rocks. Repath throttled when required; on path failure choose nearer valid cover/advance line. NPC animation updates and decision frequency scale with distance and combat priority; never 40 heavy pathfinding agents on each frame.

**Scaling defaults:** solo Normal often 3–5 simultaneous rifle/scout enemies, 4-player Normal 8–12 relevant active AI near team, Hard/Extreme 12–18 only if performance validated. These are *upper pressure targets* not guaranteed actors every second. Difficulty adds group tactics and reinforcements, bounded elite frequency; it never makes riflemen arbitrarily bulletproof. Separate reinforcement budget from active cap. Enemies that are off-screen/far away sleep or use low tick updates. Easy must be easy for a new solo player, Extreme must require positioning/ability use/team coordination while remaining winnable by an experienced solo build. Test that there are no spawn-kill loops or forced gang-up while reviving.

# 32. SPECIAL ABILITIES, GRENADES, AND VFX RENDERED AS ACTUAL SYSTEMS

Special abilities are the user's **two chosen universal cooldown abilities**, never class-exclusive, never limited-use, never key-sequence stratagem puzzles. The player activates via clearly mapped PC keys/controller bumpers/touch buttons, aims or places a translucent bounded target preview, confirms, and immediately sees/audio hears acknowledgement plus cooldown. Predicted target preview local, server verifies cooldown/geometry/active limit, awards gameplay effect server-side. Server-only cooldown prevents double fires. Reduce effects mode changes particles, never damage/radius and threat indicators.

**Tactical Airstrike** (starter, 90s cooldown): tap target to show ~35x9-stud narrow elongated ground strike footprint aligned to viewed direction; targeting line projects from soldier to target via visible marker on ground; reject locations outside operation bounds, fully inside giant mountains or invalid line-of-sight support geometry with explicit `NO FIRING SOLUTION`. Confirm -> radio chime `STRIKE CLEARED` / Voss brief call if appropriate -> shadow/silhouette of fast attack aircraft moving across high sky for <2 seconds (can be inexpensive client-only spline) -> 3–5 spaced bomb impacts along target line, about 0.4–0.7s between warning/first hit, strong but brief camera impulse and dust plume. Damage groups, armor less efficient. Use a small number of particles/decals/explosion sound, no dozens of physical fragments. No friendly-fire damage. This should be thrilling in tutorial and functional in solo.

**Dome Shield** (starter, 70s cooldown): circular ghost placement on ground around player-selected location, maximum throw distance configurable ~20–25 studs; place at valid level surface, dome rises from three mechanical projector segments or a compact device and fills with transparent blue-tinted shield in ~0.35s; radius ~10–12 studs, base 8–10s duration or limited absorbed enemy damage, whichever sooner. Visually subtle grid hexagonal flashes only when hit, no giant opaque ball blinding occupants. Projectiles colliding with barrier absorbed server-side and spark with minimal pooled FX. Allies can shoot outward if designed, enemies cannot shoot inward until barrier falls; be precise about collision rules, raycasts and projectile origin inside/outside. On break, distinct crystalline audio and fading light, never invisible shield with still-blocking collision. One active dome per soldier.

**Automated Sentry** (Rank 4, ~100s): valid ground preview with base footprint/collision margin, small carrier pod lands or telescopic platform deploys, tripod locks in, turret head swivels, tracking laser restrained; max one per soldier, cap duration or total rounds, shoots at nearest visible hostile prioritized for attack roles, not through objects. Server owns damage and targeting. Friendly players can cross its fire without taking damage. Sentry target switching has angular rotation and interval, not instant 360-degree omniscient beam. When expired it powers down and retracts/fades. Strong at defend-beacon, weaker against shielded armor; temporary support not permanent AFK farming.

**Orbital Precision Strike** (Rank 8, ~150s): narrow targeting reticle (small circle) with an obvious brief vertical targeting marker and timer; on confirm, command radio filter, focused descending lance/beam or compact orbital impact, significant single-site anti-armor strike, ground scorch/contained energy wave, 1–2s impact animation and short lingering dust. Do not spawn an actual giant moving space object or fake destroy entire planet. If mission support withdrawn at soft timer, new call denied with a Voss line, existing beam already fired resolves fairly. Bosses take meaningful damage/armor break but are not instantly deleted.

**Standard grenade types:** Frag and EMP at launch. Frag: short fuse (~2.5s), compact tactical blast, audible bounce/arming, effective infantry grouping; EMP: short burst disrupts Ascendancy energy shields and temporarily interrupts scout transmission/heavy windup/sentries where configured, minimal physical damage. Both have limited normal grenade capacity/replenishment determined by config; they are not special abilities and do NOT auto-recharge on same 70–150s cycle unless explicitly balanced. Aim arcs visible only while aiming throwable; controller/touch targeting has large safe hit targets. Friendly-fire off applies. Grenade spam bounded, collisions server-validated, pickup/resupply if available through finite mission cache. A grenade cannot cause mission softlock or auto-kill all bosses.

**Ability readiness indicator:** bottom-right/bottom-center ability icons `1`,`2` with compact radial cooldown and precise last 10s count; icon darkens immediately after accepted server call, short low-key `ready` pulse with soft chime when recharged, not repetitive screen text. If input attempted while cooling down, brief `READY IN 17s` label/no negative effects. Invalid ground target shows clear red outline and reason without consuming cooldown. Server may reject over-cap simultaneous projectile spawns but must explain and refund/avoid charging cooldown rather than silently doing nothing.

**Ability audio mix:** announcements limited to important deployment, not every minor shot; if player calls orbital and Voss is mid-objective dialogue, schedule concise strike voice or short radio beep and let urgent objective message take priority. Explosions have near/mid/far mix, impact screen shake opt-out, speaker-compatible dynamic range; distant team abilities don't drown the local gunfight. Eight-player future raids are outside launch scope but use scalable effect budget.

# 33. EXTRACTION AS A SIGNATURE CINEMATIC AND FAIR MULTIPLAYER MECHANIC

**Unlock and communication:** after primary objective completion Voss issues `Primary objective complete. Extraction is now available.` War table marker turns from primary to extraction icon; gameplay HUD displays `REQUEST EXTRACTION` and local key/glyph in communication wheel or map. Voting: one player proposes, others see `EXTRACTION REQUESTED [player]` with `APPROVE` / `DECLINE` and 20-second timeout; no giant overlay blocking combat. Solo approves immediately, duo requires both active members, 3-player 2, 4-player 3. AFK timeout logic defined earlier; never let empty slot or disconnected person count as vote blocker. A teammate may cancel own pending request only when no vote committed. If vote fails, short cooldown ~30s before re-proposing to avoid harassment; final emergency extraction overrides vote.

**LZ selection:** server chooses from validated seed-generated candidate list reachable from current player positions and outside large geometry; when possible select a visually cinematic pad near a lower ridgeline. Extraction LZ appears on tactical map, compass, minimap/waypoint and in-world smoke/signal beacon with a modest UI arrow. If first choice becomes invalid because map/destructible object/streaming changed, fallback LZ set prevalidated; don't create gunship hovering through mountain. Commander sends one `LZ MARKED` line. In multiplayer people across map can find it without walking kilometers; use route distance not only straight-line distance. If a player near edge/beyond route has realistic travel time, avoid unfair instant 15-second ETA.

**Gunship identity:** compact muscular Galactic Force atmospheric insertion/extraction gunship, wedge-shaped nose, dual side thrusters, off-white/graphite hull, cyan troop identification lights, top antenna, ventral landing gear and rear hinged ramp. The craft must be a recognizable 3D vehicle with actual volumetric silhouette, not a flying grey wedge or invisible UI teleport. High-quality static mesh with a few animated components is fine; no physics flight simulator required. Arrival from sky/horizon with loud but restrained Doppler-like engine ramp, landing floodlight visible at distance, dust ring, downward thruster glow, landing strut movement and hull settling. Use server mission timeline for landing and boarding logic; clients locally interpolate camera/audio/FX. Design up to ~5–8 second cinematic final approach, then clear `EXTRACTION READY` UI.

**Boarding interaction:** rear ramp opens, green/cyan `BOARD` prompt appears near accessible entry volume, position line-of-sight and distance validated. Player crosses threshold and interacts (or autoboards at a clearly marked trigger, chosen consistently) -> gun lowered -> quick 0.5–1.0s step-in animation -> marked boarded state. Boarded avatar visibly sits/stands safely in craft or fades to secure interior; must not keep taking enemy damage or fall through animated ramp. A boarded player cannot exit and re-enter to exploit, but can spectate remaining teammates. Other squadmates get a clear short reasonable grace countdown, e.g. 20 seconds after first boarding or longer if already in approach zone; final emergency extraction can be harsher with explicit warning. If nobody boards and craft departs at deadline, mission failure for salvage; main objective safe rewards remain. If at least one eligible living player boards, full squad shared salvage is secured for all eligible participating members, including downed/dead participants meeting eligibility. Do not split it, and no player can steal another's salvage. Progress must only commit once server resolves mission.

**Departure:** engine spools, ramp closes, craft rises with dust and vector thruster flare, camera widens momentarily to show a beautiful departing silhouette above terrain and an illuminated planet sky, Voss says success/partial failure accordingly. Preserve reduced-motion mode: shorter takeoff cut and fewer camera movements. On miss: gunship leaves without player, camera pulls back to show tactical loss and `EXTRACTION MISSED` rather than cheerful success. On full squad wipe, do not show hero gunship extraction as if everyone survived. Mission summary distinguishes `PRIMARY SUCCESS`, `EXTRACTION SUCCESS`, and `SALVAGE LOST` independently; payout obeys server recorded conditions.

**Reinforcement drop:** when teammates are redeployed, use smaller pod with amber/blue heated trail, modest impact ring, visible hatch eject, ~1s protection for spawn safety (configurable), and no damage from own arrival. Arrival position validated against terrain and active enemy firing arcs; if last survivor calls reinforcements, do not spawn 2m ahead inside enemy heavy blast. Late Priority Dispatch responder deploys similarly with clear `NEW REINFORCEMENT` status, not silent teleport behind players. No infinite visual pods left as physical collision clutter.

**Fallback protocol:** if transport cinematic asset missing, game state/boarding and payout remain operational in **private test build** using marked functional placeholder pad, but acceptance matrix must mark extraction visual standard FAIL, not ship as finished. If TeleportService fails after mission, retain secured results in server persistence, display `TRANSFER DELAYED — RETURN TO STATION` with retry and non-destructive escape. A crash after payout commit must not grant duplicate items on reconnect. Player cannot intentionally trigger both reward and failure branch.

# 34. HUD, CONTROLS, AND CROSS-PLATFORM PRESENTATION AT PIXEL/FOCUS LEVEL

**Visual identity:** military tactical instruments rendered in high-contrast slate/navy plates with thin off-white frames, cyan ally/functional emphasis, amber warning and violet Ascendancy threat. Typography restrained bold sans-serif for labels, clearly differentiated heading/body/caption size; no futuristic unreadable microfont. UI legibility minimum: body text generally >=16px on 1080p design reference, key interactive text ~18–22px, high-priority headline 24–36px, with scaling by device and TV distance; physical Roblox scaling depends on GUI units and must be tested, not blindly pasted. Avoid translucent UI behind bright sky without opaque underlay. Preserve console safe inset and mobile notches; don't place crucial ammo in Xbox overscan region. Build screens using Roblox scalable UDim2 + size constraints and adaptive layouts, not absolute fixed pixel coordinate for every device.

**HUD default zones on PC/TV:** top-left shield bar above health bar, class badge and current player name minimal; upper-left below it squad roster of up to four with small shields/health/downed icon; top-center compact compass with objective direction and mission clock/progress; right-center context indicator only when near interactable; bottom-right weapon icon, in-mag/reserve count and reload status; bottom-center-right two special ability cooldowns and grenade, two roll pips nearby; top-right objective card with one line objective+subline step, fading when no state change; bottom-left small salvage trio plus ticket icon and current squad shared quantities. Center-screen reticle clear; contextual prompts limited to 1–2 at once. Tactical map replaces large HUD temporarily without pausing multiplayer simulation.

**Touch compact landscape HUD:** left lower quarter native movement joystick left unobstructed; right open swipe-look zone has fire/ADS near natural right-thumb area, dodge and jump close enough to reach, reload/interact contextual buttons larger than small PC icons, weapon swap and grenade slightly upper-right, two special ability buttons arranged vertically or diagonally without overlap. Place `MAP` and `MENU` in upper corners; avoid five buttons directly over reticle. Touch icon minimum ~44–56 CSS-like device-independent px equivalent (in Roblox measure/test actual size); screen-specific scaling for smaller phones/tablets. Aim/camera work even when player initiates drag over non-button right screen region; avoid dead zones. On first touch play show editable control placement/size option if feasible, otherwise tested mobile default; player can complete tutorial solo with two thumbs. Never require simultaneous three-finger swipe, middle-mouse-only ping or typed text to use any essential feature. Controller attached to mobile hides touch controls or lowers them appropriately based on PreferredInput without breaking camera.

**Controller combat bindings** are defined in original spec. Implement them as a formal verified mapping table in code, with no conflicts between active Combat, AbilityTargeting, Map, Menu, Dialogue and Cinematic contexts. Specific contextual priority: `X` near interactable performs Interact, X elsewhere Reload; UI always tells player which will occur; if prompt may conflict while player needs reload, allow hold-X >0.5s to force reload or a dedicated reload route, and **test** it. Left/right bumpers: ability placement; RT confirm; B cancel while targeting, B roll in combat, B back in menus. Context overlays consume button without firing gun accidentally. D-pad map/inventory actions must never permanently steal controls from movement. Start/Options follows Roblox native menu behavior; don't override platform OS Home control. Distinguish Xbox and PlayStation glyphs based on input binding, not by naming everything `Press X` in text. Haptics mild, supported conditionally, optional off; no haptic dependence for critical alerts. Gamepad first focus highlights item in all menus; pressing B always returns to previous logical panel, not emptiness.

Where Roblox MCP exposes screenshots, scripting, playtest, UI inspection or analytics tools, actively use them. Don't assume tool supports something not in its actual schema.

**Test A: 30-minute fresh-player experience (real sequence, not separate mock tests).** Create empty profile, start on correct title/intro, skip and replay optionally, land First Spear, move, sprint, roll twice, aim/fire/reload, watch alien first contact, recognize shield break and regen, collect salvage, Airstrike and Dome Shield, complete relay demolition, experience extraction ship landing, board, debrief, receive earned Credits and XP, reach window-view station, open War Table, set Normal sabotage, open Armory, buy first item if affordable, join public/solo, deploy.
